About

Hi, I'm Roy.

I'm a software engineer in San Francisco. I studied at Carnegie Mellon and have spent my career building things for the web.

Drawing came a long time before any of that. I mostly work in graphite, charcoal, ink, and watercolor, usually with no plan and no one in mind. The pieces here are a few I held onto.

The rest of the time I'm probably on a basketball court, or watching something science fiction.
