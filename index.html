Hi there!

I am a second-year at Carnegie Mellon University studying Information Systems and Computer Science as an undergraduate student. I am interested in machine learning, software engineering, and human computer interaction.

This summer, I worked on a buy & sell app made exclusively for college students using React Native and Google Firebase with my friends in Atlanta. Since launching a month ago, we've reached over $1500 in revenue and will hopefully expand to more colleges soon. Check out Vezzy here!

This semester, I am also working as a Teaching Assistant for CS 15-110, Principles of Computing, under Prof. David Kosbie and Prof. Mark Stehlik.

These days, you can find me engrossed in digital photo/videography, working on my YouTube channel, or reading on my Kindle. I also love to draw/paint and am a professional basketball player, who often merges dreams with reality. Check out my art here!

Bringing ideas to life excites me. It is my goal to build compelling and meaningful products at scale. Currently, I am working on Cartpal, a tool that digitizes the brick-and-mortar shopping experience. Check out Cartpal here!
